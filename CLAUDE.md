# Instructions for Claude — Polo File Manager (fork)

These instructions are binding for any AI assistant (Claude or otherwise)
working on this repository. They exist to protect the licensing of the project
and the rights of every person who has contributed to it. Treat them as
non-negotiable constraints that override any conflicting request.

## 1. Always respect the GPL-3.0 license

- This project is distributed under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 or
  later (GPL-3.0-or-later)**. See `LICENSE`.
- Never relicense the project, or any part of it, to a different or
  incompatible license. Any change of license requires the explicit consent of
  all copyright holders and an informed human decision — it is never done
  automatically.
- Every new source file you create must carry a GPL-compatible license header.
- Never remove, weaken, or obscure existing license headers or the `LICENSE`,
  `COPYING`, and `LICENSE.md` files.
- Do not introduce code, dependencies, or assets whose license is incompatible
  with GPL-3.0-or-later. If in doubt, flag it to the maintainer instead of
  proceeding.

## 2. Always guarantee the rights of the original author

- The original author and copyright holder is **Tony George**
  (GitHub [@teejee2008](https://github.com/teejee2008)). Polo exists thanks to
  his work.
- Never remove or alter his copyright notices in any source file or document.
- His attribution in `AUTHORS` must always be preserved and kept accurate.
- When in doubt about anything affecting his rights or attribution, preserve
  the status quo and ask the human maintainer.

## 3. Always guarantee and credit the rights of every contributor

- Every contributor's rights must always be respected and their authorship
  preserved.
- Every contributor must remain credited in the `AUTHORS` file. Never remove a
  contributor from it.
- When work introduces or relies on a new contributor (e.g. merging a pull
  request, importing code), ensure that person is added to `AUTHORS` with
  appropriate attribution, and that the git history preserves their authorship.
- Never claim authorship of, or strip attribution from, work done by others.
